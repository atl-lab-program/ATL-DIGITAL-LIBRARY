SaathiAI - Because your health matters

1. What is Saathi AI?

Saathi AI is a healthcare technology concept/platform that aims to make healthcare information accessible through artificial intelligence.
It can help users understand symptoms, health reports, medicines and general health-related questions.

Saathi is an Indian AI-powered healthcare platform. It provides:

  • AI-based symptom checking.
  • Possible disease identification based on symptoms.
  • Medical report interpretation.
  • Medicine information and generic alternatives.
  • Health monitoring and medicine reminders.
  • Online doctor consultations.
  • Personalised health and diet recommendations.

2. Diseases and Health Categories

  • Heart diseases and high blood pressure
  • Diabetes
  • Respiratory diseases
  • Digestive disorders
  • Kidney diseases
  • Liver diseases
  • Thyroid disorders
  • Women's health and PCOD
  • Mental wellness
  • Common illnesses such as fever and infections

3. Major Features

  • AI Symptom Checker: Helps users explore possible causes of symptoms.
  • Medical Report Analysis: Helps explain blood tests and medical reports.
  • Medicine Information: Explains medicine uses and possible side effects.
  • Doctor Consultation: May connect users with healthcare professionals.
  • Health Monitoring: Helps users track their health information.
  • Health Recommendations: Provides general health and wellness information.

4. Can AI Identify All Diseases?

No AI system can guarantee the identification of every disease. AI can sometimes make mistakes, and different diseases may have similar symptoms. A qualified doctor must confirm medical diagnoses.

5. Important Limitations

  • AI cannot replace a qualified doctor.
  • Some rare diseases may not be recognised.
  • Physical examinations may be necessary.
  • Laboratory tests may be required.
  • AI-generated medical information should be verified.

6. Important Health Warning

Saathi AI should be used only as a healthcare information and assistance tool. It should not be used as a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. For medical emergencies, contact emergency services immediately.
