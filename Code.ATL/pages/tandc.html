Terms And Conditions

Terms & Conditions for Book Borrowers (Temporary Recipients)
By borrowing a book from the Exchange Library, you agree to the following:

Temporary Possession & Maximum Loan Period: 

You are receiving this book on temporary loan. Ownership remains with the original owner. You agree 
not to sell, trade, or permanently keep the book.

Maximum Borrowing Period: Borrowers are permitted to keep any borrowed book for a maximum duration of 
up to 30 days from the date of receipt. The book must be returned on or before the 30th day.

Care & Preservation:

You agree to treat the book with care.

No writing, highlighting, dog-earing pages, or eating/drinking in a way that risks staining the book.

Strict Return Deadlines & Late Fines:

You must return the book by the agreed due date (not exceeding the 30-day limit) so it can be returned 
to its owner or passed to the next borrower.

If you need an extension, you must request it before the due date, subject to the maximum allowable duration.

Late Return Fine: Failure to return the book on time will incur a fine of ₹1 per day until the book is returned or replaced.

Non-Return & Blacklisting:

Unreturned Books: Keeping a book permanently without returning it or without explicitly replacing it is 
strictly forbidden.

Blacklisting Enforcement: If fines are not paid, the borrower will be blacklisted and suspended from 
borrowing privileges until the total accrued daily fine plus an extra ₹15 fee calculated up to that 
payment date is fully paid. Refusing to settle these amounts will result in permanent blacklisting.

Loss or Major Damage Responsibility:

If a book is lost, stolen, or damaged beyond normal wear (e.g., liquid spills, torn covers, missing pages),
you agree to either replace it with a copy in equal/better condition or reimburse the owner/library for 
the replacement cost.

Cash Reimbursement Royalty: If a replacement is made via cash reimbursement instead of providing a physical
copy of the book, the library will charge an extra ₹15 royalty fee on top of the reimbursement amount to cover
processing costs.

Terms & Conditions for Book Donors (Lenders)
By listing or placing your personal book into the Exchange Library, you agree to the following:

Retained Ownership & Return Scheduling:

You retain full ownership of your book. Placing it in the exchange grants permission for registered members
to borrow and read it temporarily.

Custom Return Date: You may specify the exact date you want your book returned to you upon submission, 
provided this date is at least 1 week (7 days) after the date of donation.

Acceptance of Wear and Tear:

You acknowledge that circulating books naturally experience minor wear and tear (e.g., slight spine 
creasing, edge wear).

The library is not liable for standard usage marks accrued during normal reading.

Condition Requirements:

Books must be complete (no missing pages) and free of hazards like mold, dampness, or pests before entering 
circulation.

Please remove any personal items (photos, notes, bookmarks) prior to handing over the book.

Recall Rights:

Subject to the minimum 1-week period and your specified return timeline, you may request the return of your 
book at the end of an active borrower's lending cycle with reasonable notice through the library system.
