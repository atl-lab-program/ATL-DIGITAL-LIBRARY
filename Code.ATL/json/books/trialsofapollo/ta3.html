The Burning Maze

In The Burning Maze, the third book in Rick Riordan's The Trials of Apollo series, the mortal ex-god Lester Papadopoulos, Meg McCaffrey, and the satyr Grover Underwood enter the Labyrinth in the American Southwest to rescue the third Oracle and stop the evil Roman Emperor Caligula from burning the land and attacking Camp Jupiter.
