The Tyrant's Tomb

In The Tyrant's Tomb, the fourth book of Rick Riordan's The Trials of Apollo series, the mortal ex-god Lester Papadopoulos (Apollo) and Meg McAffrey travel to Camp Jupiter in the San Francisco Bay Area carrying the body of Jason Grace. They must help the Roman demigods prepare for a desperate battle against the evil emperors Caligula and Commodus.
