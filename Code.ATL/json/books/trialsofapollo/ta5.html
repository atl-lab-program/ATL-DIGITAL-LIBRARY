The Tower Of Nero

In The Tower of Nero, the mortal former god Lester Papadopoulos (Apollo) and Meg McCaffrey travel to New York. They defeat Meg's abusive stepfather, Emperor Nero, and finally face Apollo's arch-nemesis, the giant serpent Python, at Delphi. Apollo sacrifices himself in battle to drag Python down into Chaos, earning his restoration as a full Olympian god.
