Rabindranatah Tagore

The Amar Chitra Katha (ACK) biography titled Rabindranath Tagore provides a beautifully illustrated look into the life of the legendary polymath. Originally published in 1977 and edited by Anant Pai, the comic introduces young readers to Tagore’s creative genius, philosophical depth, and contribution to India's cultural landscape. By blending historic milestones with intimate personal anecdotes, the narrative masterfully brings his journey from a sensitive, nature-loving child to a global literary icon alive for children.
