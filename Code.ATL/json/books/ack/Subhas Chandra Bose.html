Subhas Chandra Bose

The Amar Chitra Katha historical comic titled Subhas Chandra Bose is an inspiring, illustrated tribute to one of India's most dynamic freedom fighters. Edited by Anant Pai, the narrative chronicles his journey from a brilliant student to the revolutionary leader affectionately known as 'Netaji.' By focusing on his radical stance for complete independence, the book vividly portrays his tactical genius and unyielding defiance against British colonial rule.
