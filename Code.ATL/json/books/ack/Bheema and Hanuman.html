Bheema and Hanuman

The Amar Chitra Katha comic Bheema and Hanuman narrates the legendary meeting between two mighty sons of Vayu, the wind god, from different cosmic eras. Set during the Mahabharata, the story beautifully blends humility, brotherly affection, and divine strength. It serves as a classic lesson on overcoming ego, as the powerful Pandava prince Bheema encounters his older, immortal brother, Hanuman, disguised as an frail old monkey.
