Diary of a Wimpy Kid: The Last Straw by Jeff Kinney follows Greg Heffley as he navigates a new school semester while trying to avoid his father's attempts to toughen him up.
Frank Heffley, tired of Greg's lazy and unathletic lifestyle, threatens to send him to a strict military academy if he does not change his ways.
