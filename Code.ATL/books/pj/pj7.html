Percy Jackson 6

Wrath of the Triple Goddess by Rick Riordan follows senior high schooler Percy Jackson as he tries to earn his second college recommendation letter for New Rome University.
To secure it, Percy, Annabeth, and Grover must house-sit and pet-sit for the goddess Hecate over Halloween week.
However, the seemingly simple chore quickly turns disastrous when Hecate's ancient, magical pets escape into New York City, leaving the trio to hunt them down before the goddess returns.
