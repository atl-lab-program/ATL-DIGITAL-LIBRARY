Percy Jackson 4

The Battle of the Labyrinth is the high-stakes fourth installment in Rick Riordan's Percy Jackson & the Olympians series.
As the final war against the Titans looms, Percy and his friends must navigate a deadly, shape-shifting underground maze to stop a catastrophic invasion of Camp Half-Blood.
This pivotal adventure deepens the conflict and tests the characters' courage and loyalties.
