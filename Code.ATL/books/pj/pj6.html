Percy Jackson 6

In The Chalice of the Gods by Rick Riordan, a 17-year-old Percy Jackson tries to navigate a normal senior year of high school while embarking on a quest to secure three divine letters of recommendation required for admission into New Rome University.
This sixth installment of the original series reunites the classic trio of Percy, Annabeth, and Grover as they search for the missing magical goblet of Ganymede, the cupbearer of the gods, before a mortal drinks from it and gains unintended immortality.
